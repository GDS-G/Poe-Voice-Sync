Poe Voice Sync Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 01/02/2024

1. Introduction

Poe Voice Sync is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when using our Google Chrome Extension.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personally Identifiable Information: We collect your email address when you sign in using Google authentication. This is used to associate your license with your account.
  • Payment Information: Payment transactions for license activation are securely processed via PayPal. We do not store or process your credit card information.
  • Settings Data: API keys, voice preferences, and volume settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's sync storage.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide and improve the functionality of the extension. This includes:

  • Authenticating users and verifying licenses.
  • Configuring text-to-speech settings based on user preferences.
  • Facilitating secure payment transactions for license activation.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, except as necessary to provide core functionality (e.g., using ElevenLabs API for text-to-speech services and PayPal for payment processing).

5. Data Storage and Security

We use Chrome's secure sync storage to store user data such as API keys and settings. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Your Choices

You have the right to:

  • View and update your settings via the extension popup.
  • Revoke Google authentication at any time through your Google account settings.
  • Contact us to delete any associated data if you stop using the extension.

7. Third-Party Services

This extension interacts with the following third-party services:

  • ElevenLabs: Used to convert text to speech. API calls include the text to be converted and user-specified voice settings.
  • PayPal: Used for secure payment processing.
  • Google OAuth: Used to authenticate users and retrieve their email addresses for license association.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@poevoicesync.com.
